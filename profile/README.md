# OpenProof

### Source-linked evidence for decisions that must remain reviewable.

**OpenProof by TruthX** helps people examine complex document sets without losing the thread between a finding, its source, the elements that challenge it, and the human decision that follows.

## A case that can be verified. A report that can be handed over.

OpenProof is being developed for legal professionals and other teams examining complex cases in operations, audit, compliance and research.

It is designed to preserve a reviewable chain:

**documents → claims → source passages → contradictions and gaps → human review → handover**

The objective is not to automate judgment. It is to make the reasoning behind a decision easier to inspect, challenge and transmit.

## Explore something concrete

### Three cofounders. One disputed history.

Our public walkthrough is a fully fictional case: three cofounders, disputed spending, lost customers, conflicting evidence and a financial claim.

- [Examine the reconstructed case](https://openproof.net/docs/case-brief.html)

The findings are prewritten for the demonstration. The example does **not** claim to show automatic contradiction detection or establish liability.

## Contribute

You do not need a GitHub account to propose a contribution.

**Primary path — [share your idea through the OpenProof form](https://openproof.net/?interest=research&utm_campaign=first-contributors&utm_medium=contribute-section&utm_source=openproof-site)**  
Choose *Research / contribution*, describe the observation or contribution you would like to make, and do not send confidential documents.

**Optional GitHub exercise — [review one claim in 15 minutes](https://github.com/openproof-net/openproof-rpo/issues/44)**  
Identify what the fictional sources support, what challenges the claim, and what remains missing. Reading is open; a GitHub account is only needed to reply.

More ways to help:

- [Start here: contribution orientation](https://github.com/openproof-net/openproof-rpo/issues/43)

## Open technology

The public **Registered Probative Object (RPO) v0.1** is a structured record format for keeping claims, sources, reservations and review decisions connected.

- [RPO v0.1 repository](https://github.com/openproof-net/openproof-rpo)
- [Specification guide](https://openproof.net/docs/spec.html)
- [Local integrity exercise](https://github.com/openproof-net/openproof-rpo/blob/main/START_HERE.md)
- [Community and contribution paths](https://github.com/openproof-net/openproof-rpo/blob/main/COMMUNITY.md)

The public repository and its licence apply only to the material contained there. The complete OpenProof application and TruthX Engine are not currently released as open-source software.

## What exists today

**Public and examinable**

- a bilingual website and fictional cofounder case;
- a draft RPO v0.1 specification;
- fictional reference examples;
- a local structural-integrity checker;
- public contribution exercises and review threads.

**In development**

- the OpenProof application and document-review workflow;
- progressive integration and evaluation of the research foundation;
- pilot use with human review and explicit acceptance decisions.

Purchases and subscriptions are not open. A matching fingerprint can show that content changed; it cannot establish source authenticity, factual truth or legal validity.

## Research foundation

OpenProof builds on research collaboration with **GREYC / Université de Caen Normandie** during 2025–2026.

Under **Professor Gaël Dias's** academic supervision, **Lucy Martin** and **Clément Correia-Peltier** developed a multi-agent research prototype and delivered code, a report and a presentation in May 2026. The prototype is a research foundation; its integration into the current engine still needs to be implemented and evaluated.

[Research contributions and credits](https://github.com/openproof-net/openproof-rpo/blob/main/RESEARCH_COLLABORATION.md)
